3WRITE

Think of it like a master recording. You mix it once, then export different formats.

Every master version has four parts:

PART WHAT IT DOES
HOOK The first line. Stops the scroll or nothing else matters.
BODY The actual thing you're teaching or telling.
VALUE The specific takeaway they can use today.
CTA One clear ask. Only one.

Write the master version of this content. This is the full version. Everything else gets cut down from it. TOPIC: [your topic] AUDIENCE: [who this is for] TONE: [direct and punchy/ warm and friendly/ dry and funny] GOAL: [educate/engage/sell/grow] CTA: [your one ask] Structure it exactly like this: HOOK One line. Make me stop scrolling. Give me 3 options. BODY The core content. Specific, not vague. Use real examples, real numbers, real situations. 150-250 words. 4ADAPT

6SCHEDULE

One idea gives you a full week. Here's how to spread it so you're not dumping everything at once.

DAY WHAT GOES OUT
MONDAY Instagram Carousel / LinkedIn Post / X Post
TUESDAY TikTok / YouTube Short / Threads
WEDNESDAY Instagram Reel / Pinterest / Reddit
THURSDAY X thread / Threads follow-up on whatever got replies
FRIDAY Repost your best performer of the week to Stories
WEEKEND Off. Or reply to comments. That's it.

Three ways to actually schedule it:

  1. Native schedulers - built into most platforms, free, no third party involved. Slowest, but zero setup and zero risk.
  2. Approved scheduling tools - one dashboard, many platforms. Easiest option for most people. Coverage varies by tool, so check before you pay.
  3. Custom integrations - only where a platform offers an official API. Most powerful, most setup. Skip this until the rest is working.
IMPORTANT Platforms do not all support the same automation. Some allow full API publishing. Some allow scheduling only through approved partners. Some require you to post manually. This changes regularly — always check the current rules for your platform before you build around it, and use official APIs and approved tools rather than anything that automates a logged-in browser session. That's how accounts get restricted.

7PUBLISH

CONTENT READY → REVIEW → APPROVE → SCHEDULE → PUBLISH
IMPORTANT — NON-NEGOTIABLE DEFAULT HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED = TRUE

The system should never publish without you reading it first. Not because AI is bad at writing — because it's your name on the post. One weird sentence, one wrong claim, one tone-deaf take going out at the wrong moment costs you more than the ten seconds a review takes. Keep the human in the loop. Always.

Give every piece of content a status. Track it in a spreadsheet or Notion — one row per post:

STATUS MEANS
DRAFT AI wrote it, nobody's looked yet
READY Finished and waiting for your review
APPROVED You read it. You're happy. Cleared to go.
SCHEDULED Queued with a date and time locked in
PUBLISHED Live. Confirmed live, not assumed live.
FAILED Something broke. Needs you. Never silently retried.
IMPORTANT If a post can't be published automatically, the correct status is READY or FAILED — never PUBLISHED. A system that quietly marks things as posted when they weren't is worse than no system at all, because you stop checking.

IMPORTANT Not every platform gives you every metric. Some show saves, some don't. Some show watch time, some show a vague "average view duration." Some hide almost everything unless you have a business account. Record what you actually have and mark the rest as unavailable — don't guess, and don't let the AI fill gaps with invented numbers.

POST → TRACK → LEARN → IMPROVE
