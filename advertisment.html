HOW IT PRACTICALLY WORKS

A week of content in under 15 minutes of your time

1
You type four lines
Topic, audience, goal, CTA. That's the entire input. Example: "3 AI automations that save time / creators / educate / comment AUTO"
2
AI writes the master version
Hook (3 options), body, value, CTA. The full-fat version of your idea. You pick your favorite hook.
3
AI adapts for 8 platforms
Each platform gets its own hook, its own tone, its own format. LinkedIn reads like LinkedIn. TikTok reads like TikTok. Never the same post twice.
4
Captions + schedule generated
Platform-specific captions using proven formulas. A day-by-day week plan with optimal posting times based on your past performance.
5
You review & approve
Nothing goes live without your sign-off. Edit inline, reject what doesn't fit, approve what's ready. Takes ~5 minutes.
6
System publishes + learns
Auto-publishes where APIs allow. Manual prompts where they don't. Tracks results. Feeds learnings back into next week's content.
Honest by design: We only use official APIs and approved scheduling tools. No browser automation. No silent failures. If a post can't publish, you'll know — it's marked FAILED, never secretly marked PUBLISHED.
